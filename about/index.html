About

I like making difficult things a little easier to see.

This is D.797's personal home. It will slowly gather notes on physics, code, interfaces, and the act of thinking itself.

There is no fixed schedule and no attempt to explain everything. Think of it as an open sheet of paper: finished thoughts are written down, and unfinished ones are allowed to keep their space.
